## 🇮🇩 **Big Idea: ID-Kita — Indonesia’s Digital Identity Super Wallet**

### 🔐 Vision:
To create **a secure, portable, and universal identity platform** that enables every Indonesian to access public and private services anytime, anywhere — without paperwork or physical cards.

---

## 💼 What is ID-Kita?
A mobile-first digital wallet that holds **digitized, verified versions of a citizen’s key identity documents and credentials**, protected by multi-layer authentication and usable both **online and offline**.

---

## 🧱 Core Components of ID-Kita

### 1. 📱 **Digital Identity Vault**
Securely stores:
- e-KTP (with facial recognition biometrics)
- Family Card (KK)
- NPWP (Tax ID)
- Driver's License (SIM)
- BPJS Health and Employment Cards
- Passport (linked to immigration)
- Voter ID (KPU integration)

🔒 All data encrypted and accessible through biometric authentication (Face + Fingerprint)

---

### 2. ✍️ **Digital Sign & Consent**
- e-Signature for contracts and government forms  
- Digital consent logs (e.g., data sharing approvals)
- Stamp duty integration (e-meterai API)

💡 No more printing, scanning, or physical approvals

---

### 3. 🌐 **e-Government Access Hub**
Use ID-Kita to:
- Register businesses (OSS)
- Pay taxes
- Apply for land certificates (BPN)
- Access court, education, or healthcare records

💼 **One login for all government services** (SSO for BUMNs, Kementerian, Pemda)

---

### 4. 💳 **Credential & Certificate Wallet**
Holds:
- University diplomas
- Professional licenses (e.g., medical, engineer, lawyer)
- BPOM, halal, and business certifications
- COVID vaccine records

🎓 Use blockchain-based timestamping to make all credentials tamper-proof

---

### 5. 💸 **Financial Identity Layer**
Optional integrations:
- Open Finance API (Dukcapil + BI + OJK)
- Credit score visibility (integrate with PEFINDO/SLIK)
- Bank & eWallet linking (BRI, BCA, DANA, GoPay, etc.)

🪙 Helps the **unbanked** and **underbanked** access financial services

---

### 6. 🧭 **Offline Mode & QR Scan**
- Works offline in remote areas using QR + OTP SMS fallback
- NFC card option for citizens without smartphones
- Digital KTP card that can be scanned for KYC and service access

🛰️ Super useful in eastern Indonesia or rural Java where coverage is weak

---

### 7. 👥 **Guardian & Family Mode**
- Add dependents (e.g., child, elderly parent) under your wallet
- Manage services on their behalf (BPJS claims, school admissions)

👵 Increases digital inclusion for the elderly and disabled

---

## 🎯 **Start with This: “ID-Kita for BPJS + e-KTP + SIM Access” Pilot in One City**

---

### 🧩 Why This Combo?
- **e-KTP** is already mandatory — leverage existing biometric data from Dukcapil.
- **BPJS (Health Insurance)** has massive reach — 200+ million members need digital access to services and claims.
- **SIM (Driver License)** renewal and verification is a hassle — digital ID can ease police + citizen workflows.

This combo touches **health**, **mobility**, and **legal identity** — critical daily pain points that affect trust and quality of life.

---

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Low Budget Strategy

### 1. 🔍 **Choose the Pilot Region**
Look for a city or regency that is:
- **Digitally progressive** (like Yogyakarta, Bandung, Surabaya)
- Has a good relationship between local Dukcapil, BPJS, and Polri (SIM unit)
- Open to innovation & pilot programs

---

### 2. 🛠️ **Build a Lightweight MVP App**
This app can:
- Log in with NIK + selfie verification (Dukcapil DB)
- Show verified BPJS info (class, facility, status)
- Show SIM info (expiry, class, vehicle type)
- Allow **QR-based identity scan** (for clinics or police checkpoints)
- Has **offline support** for field use

✅ Use **React Native** or **Flutter** for fast and cheap development  
✅ Use Firebase/Auth0 for security and login  
✅ Use Dukcapil sandbox API or simulate with test data first

---

### 3. 🤝 **Partner with 1 Clinic + 1 Polres Office**
Let people:
- **Use ID-Kita** for BPJS check-in at the clinic
- **Use it to show SIM** when pulled over or during road checks

Bonus:
- Add BPJS e-claim integration for referrals or reimbursements
- Add Satpas (SIM Office) digital queueing system linked to ID-Kita

---

### 4. 📊 **Measure & Promote Impact**
Track:
- Reduction in wait times
- Number of successful digital logins
- User satisfaction (farmers, ojol drivers, students, etc.)
- Feedback from Polri and BPJS staff

📣 Promote the story as a **“Digital Identity in Action”** case study for Indonesia.
