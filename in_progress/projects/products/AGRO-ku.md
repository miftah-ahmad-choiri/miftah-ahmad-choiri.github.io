## ✅ MVP Blueprint

### 🌱 **Core Idea:**
An Android app + WhatsApp bot that helps farmers in one district grow **a high-value crop better** (e.g., chili in Brebes, garlic in Lombok, or shallot in Enrekang)

---

### 🔧 **What You Need (Low Cost Stack):**
| Component            | Tools/Tech                             |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------|
| App or Bot           | Android (Kotlin) + WhatsApp API (Twilio) |
| Weather & Soil Data  | BMKG API + Free satellite data (Sentinel-2) |
| Crop Model           | Partner with a local agriculture polytechnic (for planting schedule & pest logic) |
| Language             | Bahasa + optional local dialect voice/audio |
| Hosting              | Firebase or Supabase (cheap, scalable) |

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Guide

### **1. Choose Region & Crop**
Pick a district where:
- There’s an active farming community
- Local agri co-op or BUMDes wants to collaborate
- You can visit or survey easily

🔁 *Example*: 
- Chili in Brebes
- Shallot in Enrekang
- Rice in Banyuwangi

---

### **2. Validate Problem with Real Farmers**
Do quick interviews or FGD:
- What crop are they planting?
- What are their biggest problems? (pests, rain, fertilizer?)
- How do they currently get info?

📋 Build a simple use-case:  
“Hi Pak/Bu, would you use an app that tells you when and how to plant, and warns about disease or weather?”

---

### **3. Build MVP (Max 2–3 Months)**
Build the simplest version of:
- **"Tanam Kapan & Gimana"** guide (calendar + visual/audio guide)
- **WhatsApp reminders** for pest spraying, watering, and harvesting
- **Basic AI photo scan** for leaf disease (can use free TensorFlow model)

---

### **4. Field Test with 50–100 Farmers**
Launch it in a controlled village with real farmers.
Track:
- Before/after yield
- Farmer feedback
- App engagement

---

### **5. Use Results to Get Funding or Partners**
With real field data, you can:
- Apply for grants (e.g., from Telkom, BRI, Kemendikbud, or even World Bank)
- Pitch to agriculture co-ops or government programs
- Attract startup incubators like GnB Accelerator, Indigo, or East Ventures
